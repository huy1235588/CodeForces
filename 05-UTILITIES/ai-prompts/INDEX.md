## 🔧 Customization Guide

### Variable Substitution
Replace these placeholders trong prompts:
- `{SKILL_LEVEL}`: Your current level (beginner/intermediate/advanced/expert)
- `{LANGUAGE}`: Programming language (C++/Python/Java/etc.)
- `{PLATFORM}`: Contest platform (Codeforces/AtCoder/LeetCode/etc.)
- `{TOPIC}`: Specific algorithm hoặc concept
- `{RATING}`: Your current contest rating
- `{TIME_AVAILABLE}`: Study/practice time available

### Personal Context
Add these details for better results:
- Your background và experience
- Specific goals và deadlines
- Preferred learning style
- Known strengths và weaknesses

## 📊 Effectiveness Tips

### Getting Better Responses
1. **Be Specific**: Include exact problem statements, error messages, code snippets
2. **Provide Context**: Mention your skill level, time constraints, specific goals
3. **Ask Follow-ups**: Request examples, clarifications, alternative approaches
4. **Iterate**: Refine prompts based on response quality

### Common Patterns
- **Role-based**: "Act as an expert competitive programming coach..."
- **Task-specific**: "Help me understand/debug/optimize..."
- **Format-driven**: "Provide step-by-step/comparison/analysis..."
- **Example-driven**: "Show examples of/demonstrate/illustrate..."
