## Usage Tips

### Variables to Replace
- `{SKILL_LEVEL}`: beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert
- `{LANGUAGE}`: C++, Python, Java, JavaScript, etc.
- `{PLATFORM}`: Codeforces, AtCoder, LeetCode, etc.
- `{TIME_AVAILABLE}`: Specific time amounts
- `{CURRENT_RATING}`: Your contest rating
- `{TOPIC}`: Specific algorithm/concept
- `{PROBLEM_TYPE}`: DP, Graph, Math, etc.

### Quick Selection Guide
- **Learning something new**: Use Learning Session templates
- **Stuck on problem**: Use Problem Approach hoặc Debug templates
- **Contest day**: Use Contest Day templates
- **Daily practice**: Use Quick Reference templates
- **Research mode**: Use Research templates

### Customization
- Add your specific context
- Include relevant constraints
- Specify your preferred learning style
- Mention time limitations
- Include current skill level
